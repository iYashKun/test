How the 3D Camera works

Pointer Lock hides the cursor and gives relative mouse movement (movementX / movementY).
We use that to update yaw (left/right) and pitch (up/down).
WASD moves the camera in the direction it is facing.
This is the same technique used in browser-based first-person games.
