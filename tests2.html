KeyboardEvent.code Explained

event.code represents the physical key on the keyboard. It does not change when you press Shift or change keyboard layout.

Example:
• Physical key A → always "KeyA"
• Whether you type "a" or "A" → event.key changes, event.code stays "KeyA"
